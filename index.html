Last Updated: December 6, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to GlowUp ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By using GlowUp, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

Note: Subscription purchases are processed through the Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android). Your payment information is handled by Apple or Google, and we do not have access to your payment card details.

4.1 Data Storage

4.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption of data in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Secure password hashing and encryption

  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Firebase Crashlytics for monitoring and fixing security issues
  • Access controls and authentication requirements

4.3 Data Retention

  • Active account data: Retained while your account is active

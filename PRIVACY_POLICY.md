# Privacy Policy for GlowUp

**Last Updated:** December 6, 2025

## Introduction

Welcome to GlowUp ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By using GlowUp, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Account Registration Information:**
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Password (encrypted and securely stored)
- Profile photo (optional)

**Profile Information:**
- User preferences and settings
- Subscription status and payment history
- Content preferences

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information

**Usage Data:**
- Course progress and completion status
- Video viewing history and watch time
- Saved quotes and favorite content
- Search queries and browsing history within the App
- Feature usage patterns and interaction data

**Device Information:**
- Device type and model
- Operating system version (iOS/Android)
- Unique device identifiers
- Mobile network information
- App version
- Device language settings

**Technical Data:**
- IP address
- Log files and crash reports
- Performance metrics
- API request/response data

### 1.3 Data from Third-Party Services

**Firebase Services:**
- Crash analytics and error reports (Firebase Crashlytics)
- Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
- Authentication data

**Payment Information:**
- Payment transactions are processed through Apple App Store (for iOS) and Google Play Store (for Android)
- We do NOT store your credit card or payment method information
- Payment information is handled entirely by Apple and Google
- We only receive confirmation of successful/failed transactions and subscription status from the app stores

### 1.4 Permissions We Request

**Camera (Optional):**
- Used only for taking profile pictures
- You can deny this permission and still use the App
- Access only when you explicitly choose to take a photo

**Photo Library (Optional):**
- Used only for selecting profile pictures from your device
- You can deny this permission and still use the App
- Access only when you explicitly choose to upload a photo

**Notifications (Optional):**
- Used to send you course updates, new content alerts, and important announcements
- You can disable notifications in your device settings at any time

**Internet Access (Required):**
- Necessary for app functionality including content streaming, authentication, and data synchronization

- Understanding user behavior to enhance features

### 2.4 Security and Fraud Prevention
- Detecting and preventing unauthorized access

### 2.5 Legal Compliance
- Complying with legal obligations and regulations
- Enforcing our Terms of Service

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

### 3.1 Service Providers
We work with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the App:

- **Firebase (Google):** For authentication, push notifications, crash analytics, and cloud storage
- **Apple App Store:** For subscription processing and management on iOS devices
- **Google Play Store:** For subscription processing and management on Android devices
- **API Hosting Provider:** For backend infrastructure and data storage

These service providers have access to your information only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated to protect your information.

### 3.2 Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to:
- Valid legal processes (subpoenas, court orders)
- Government or law enforcement requests
- Protection of our legal rights
- Prevention of fraud or security threats
- Protection of user safety

### 3.3 Business Transfers
In the event of a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

### 3.4 With Your Consent
We may share your information with third parties when you provide explicit consent.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Data Storage
- Your data is stored on secure servers provided by our hosting partners
- Profile information and preferences are stored locally on your device using encrypted storage
- Authentication tokens are securely stored and automatically refreshed
- We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law

### 4.2 Security Measures
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:
- Encryption of data in transit using HTTPS/TLS
- Secure password hashing and encryption
- Token-based authentication with automatic refresh
- Regular security audits and updates
- Firebase Crashlytics for monitoring and fixing security issues
- Access controls and authentication requirements

### 4.3 Data Retention
- Active account data: Retained while your account is active
- Deleted account data: Removed within 30 days of account deletion
- Legal requirements: Some data may be retained longer to comply with legal obligations
- Analytics data: May be retained in anonymized form for research and improvement
